## People (0277)

Everything points at `kh_person`, never at a user: a payer, a share, a payment's two ends, a member, a friend, an actor.

- **A user's own person** is made on first use with their `kh_profile` (`ensurePerson`, called by `resolveMe` in every service). A verified account takes over the person someone already made for its address, so the balances waiting for it appear. An unverified account or a guest never claims an address.
- **A friend** is added by name only (a contact, private to whoever added it) or by address (one person per address, shared by everyone who adds it). The caller's own name for them becomes their nickname, so neither the account's name nor whether anyone else added the address shows; whether the person has joined Kharcha does (`hasAccount`), which decides between "invite" and nothing. Guests add names only. The contact's creator can add an address later (`setFriendEmail`): if someone already has it, the contact merges into them and the creator's name for it stays as their nickname.
- **Friends both ways:** adding someone or the first debt between two people makes `kh_friend` rows in both directions. The nickname and `hidden` are each owner's own.
- **`mergePerson(tx, from, to)`** makes two people one: the guest claim, an address added to a contact, and joining (an invite's link or a verified sign-in). It adds up where both were on an expense (the split becomes the exact shares it gave; the stayer's category and method win, else the other's), drops a debt or a payment between the two, keeps one membership per group, merges their lists (`mergeLists`: the same built-in key or name is one category, the same name one tag or method; the rest, the rules and the drafts move), and moves every other reference. A table that points at people adds its lines there; `kharcha-people.test.ts` reads every foreign key to `kh_person` from the catalogue and fails if one is left pointing at the merged person.
- **When an account goes** (`forgetUser` in Better Auth's `beforeDelete`): what was theirs alone goes (personal expenses and their receipts, groups and expenses with only them and their own contacts, those contacts, their friend list, their lists and receipt drafts); what they shared stays, under their name, without the account (`user_id` set null). Nothing of Kharcha's cascades from `user_id` except the profile.

## Money (0278)

- **Amounts** are integer minor units of their own currency, end to end: `toMinor(amount, code)` where a form reads what was typed, `formatMoney(minor, code)` where it is shown, `money(code)` to validate. Never add major units.
- **An expense** has an amount, a currency, its payers (together the amount) and its shares (together the amount). `split` keeps how the shares were entered, for the editor. A refund is its own kind: money back, negative spending, its debts the other way.
- **Splits:** `computeShares(amount, split)` gives exact shares or a problem (`left to assign`, percentages not 100%…); `splitProblemMessage` says it in words, for the editor and for the server's refusal. Ties in rounding go to whoever was entered first, so the same input always gives the same shares.
- **The ledger:** `writeLedger(tx, { expense })` or `({ payment })` rewrites the source's rows from what is stored, in the write's transaction; a source in the trash has none. A payment from X to Y writes "Y owes X".
- **Balances** are sums over the ledger per currency: between the caller and each person (`pairBalances`), in a group (`groupNets`), the caller's per group (`netsByGroup`). "You're owed" and "you owe" on Home are sums over people, not a net: being owed by Asha doesn't pay what you owe Bikash.
- **Simplify** (a group setting, on by default) suggests the fewest payments per currency; it never changes a record.
- **Another currency:** a payment settles the debt in the debt's currency, and may note what changed hands (`paid`: currency, amount, the typed rate as text). Nothing is converted on its own.

## Access (0277)

| Caller | Personal expense | Shared, no group | A group and what is in it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Its owner or someone on it | everything | everything | — |
| A current group member | — | — | everything; removing members and archiving are the owner's |
| A former member | — | their own, as before | the history up to leaving, read only |
| Anyone else, guests and admins | NOT_FOUND | NOT_FOUND | NOT_FOUND |

- Every service resolves first: `resolveExpense`, `resolvePayment`, `resolveGroup`, `resolveFriend`. FORBIDDEN carries a reason in the caller's language (archived, left the group, in the trash, the owner's).
- **Who an expense may name:** in a group, its current members; outside one, the caller and their friends, and the caller must be on it.
- **Leaving or removing** needs a zero balance in the group (FORBIDDEN, with the amounts). A change to an old expense or payment that would leave a former member owing or owed is refused until they are added back. The owner leaving hands the group to the longest-standing member with an account.
- **The trash:** a deleted expense or payment can be restored for 30 days by anyone who could edit it.
- **Admins:** `kharcha:read` and `kharcha:configure` open `/admin/kharcha` (Task 96), counts only, never anyone's data (0186).
- **The matrix** is `tests/server/kharcha-access.test.ts`: every operation, as the owner, a friend, a member, a former member, a stranger, a guest and an admin. A new service function adds its operation there.

## Server patterns

- **Every write is one transaction:** resolve access, make the change, `writeLedger`, `recordActivity`, `bumpRevs` for everyone the change touches (the people on it and the group's members), `produce` the notifications (Task 92); the instant ones go to `services.deliver` after the commit (`deliverAfter`).
- **Forms save with `version`** (`saveVersioned`): a save from an older version is a stale CONFLICT. Deleting and restoring are guarded by the row's own state, so two people deleting at once leave one line of history.
- **Freshness:** `kh_profile.rev` goes up for everyone a change touches; the shell polls `getKharchaRev` every 15 s while the page is visible and on focus, and a moved `rev` refetches every query under `kharchaKeys.user(userId)`. A page's queries go under that key. The same answer carries the bell's unread count (`{ rev, unread }`, Task 92). No websockets (0209).
- **History** (`kh_activity`): a group's thread (`group_id`), the thread of two people (`person_lo` < `person_hi`), or only its expense or payment. An edit writes one row per changed field (`{ field, from, to }`; who paid and the split as a whole). Who it was done to is `subject_id`, never a key in `data`, so merging people is an indexed update.
- **Quotas** (`assertQuota`, under `lockQuota`): expenses, payments, groups, members per group and friends per person, from `kh_settings` (defaults in `settings.ts`), smaller for guests. The admin changes them in Task 96.
- **Currencies:** a new expense, payment or group must be in a currency the admin offers (`kh_settings`; empty means all of `CURRENCIES`); what exists keeps its own.

## Lists (0283)

- **Each person's own:** categories (`kh_category`: a built-in `key` or none, name unique in any case, colour, icon, order, archived), tags, payment methods (labels: no money moves) and merchant rules. Nobody else sees them; `ownCategory`, `ownTags`, `ownMethod`, `ownRule` refuse another's with NOT_FOUND.
- **On an expense,** the caller's category is on their share, their tags in `kh_share_tag`, their method on their payer row. A shared expense carries each person's own; `replaceParts` keeps them when the split changes.
- **Made on first use** (`ensureLists`, run by `getLists`): the admin's default categories (`kh_settings.categories`) and `DEFAULT_METHODS`, once (`kh_profile.lists_at`), named in the language of that visit. A guest's sample makes them at once.
- **Deleting** a category leaves its shares uncategorized and its rules without one (`set null`); a tag comes off every share (`cascade`) and is skipped in rules (`tag_ids` has no foreign key); archiving keeps it on what has it and out of the pickers.
- **Every change answers with all the lists** (`KharchaLists`), with how many of the caller's shares use each; Settings and the dialog redraw from that.
- **Settings** `/settings/$tab`: categories, tags, methods, rules (Task 89); profile (Task 91), notifications (Task 92); recurring (Task 94).

## Expenses, receipts and Spend (0283)

## Spend's search (0287)

- **The language:** words and "quoted phrases" search the title, merchant and notes (`kh_expense.search`, `simple`, as prefixes); fields are `type:` (expense, refund, payment), `category:`, `tag:`, `merchant:` (contains) and `merchant=` (exact), `amount>` `<` `=` `>=` `<=` (the expense's amount in major units), `currency:`, `date:` (today, yesterday, this-week, last-week, this-month, last-month, this-year, last-year, `YYYY`, `YYYY-MM`, `YYYY-MM-DD`; `date>`… for ranges; weeks start on Sunday), `group:`, `with:` and `paid-by:` (a person's name, its first word, the caller's nickname, or `me`), `method:`, `has:receipt`, `is:shared|personal|unsettled|duplicate|uncategorized`. `-` negates, commas mean OR. Field names are English in both languages; values are the caller's own names, matched in any case.
- **A new field** is a case in `fieldPair` (`compile.ts`, one condition for an expense share and one for a payment), its operators in `FIELD_OPS`, its words in `FIELD_WORDS` (`ast.ts`), its check in `check.ts`, and a line in the compiler's test. Nothing else reaches SQL: values are parameters.
- **The bar** (`FilterBar` with `transform`): the search box (`QueryInput`, a combobox: suggestions of fields, the closed words and the caller's names; Enter or Tab takes one, a value searches at once), the type, category and date controls (each shows what `q` says of its field; changing one rewrites it in `q`, `foldControls`), and the order. Chips are `q`'s terms, each a link without it; a term the language can't use is marked, named and left out. Without JavaScript the form sends `?q=…&category=…` and `spendQuery` folds it.
- **Views** (`?view=`): the list by day (payments as their cards when `type:payment` asks), a table (`DataTable`: its Columns menu, sorting, and bulk actions on the selection: a category, a tag, the method, the trash, a CSV), a chart (`?groupBy=` category, merchant, tag, method, group, friend, day, week, month or year; `?chart=` bar, donut or line), one per currency, the caller's share summed, the largest 12 of a list then Other (the donut 5 then Other, the kit's colours in order).
- **The header** names the search when it is a saved one, steps through months when `q` names one, and offers Save search, and in More: Export CSV, Import CSV and the saved searches' dialog.
- **Saved searches** (`kh_saved_search`, 30 each): the built-in four (`BUILT_IN_SEARCHES`, in code), then the caller's own in their order, on `me.searches`; Spend shows them as a column beside the results on a laptop and a row on a phone. The dialog renames in place, moves with buttons or Alt and an arrow on a row, and deletes.
- **CSV:** the export (`EXPORT_COLUMNS`, headers in the page's language) has the date, type, title, merchant, the caller's category, tags and method, the currency, the amount, the caller's share, the group, who paid and the notes, as plain digits. `/import` takes up to 1,000 lines: columns guessed from any language's headers, a preview through `importRowSchema`, then the valid rows once per key (`kh_import`): personal expenses, lists matched in any case and made, a negative amount with no type a refund.

## Invites and joining (0284)

- **An invite** (`kh_invite`) is a link for a friend with an address and no account (`FriendView.canInvite`), from a group (its name shows on the page) or not: `/app/kharcha/join/<token>`, 32 characters of base64url; only the token's SHA-256 is stored. Single use, 14 days; a newer link from the same sender to the same person stops the older one. Made with the friend's dialog (copy the link, email it when asked) or with "Email them an invite" when adding a friend or an address.
- **Limits:** guests send none; 20 a day per sender (`INVITE_LIMIT`, the shared rate limit); one invite email a day per address, whoever sends it (the link is made either way: `InviteResult.sent` says); `kh_settings.invites` turns them off, `kh_profile.invites_blocked_at` stops one sender (Task 96).
- **The email** (`kharcha-invite`) is in the sender's language, sent after the commit by `services.sendInvite` (tests pass their own; the default, `sendInviteEmail`, sends in the background).
- **`/join/$token`** (public, noindex) reads `getKharchaInvite` (`join.ts`, 30 lookups per visitor in 10 minutes): the sender, the group, open, used or expired; never the address or a balance. A visitor signs up or in and comes back; a guest signs up first; an account joins after a confirm ("Join as …", `acceptKharchaInvite`). Refused for the sender, a guest, a used or expired link, and once someone joined as that person.
- **Joining** merges the invited person into the account's (`mergePerson`), so everything waiting for them is theirs; on a verified sign-in, `joinOnSignIn` (in `ON_SIGN_IN`, Better Auth's `session.create.after`) does the same for a person waiting under the account's address. Both mark the invites taken and write "joined" lines in the thread with each sender and in the invite's group.

## People and threads (0284)

- **People** `/people` (`listKharchaPeople`): the caller's groups, then friends, each with its balance (`BalancePill`), searched by `?q=` (any case), hidden friends listed with `?hidden`. Each row opens its thread.
- **A thread** is read from what exists (`threads/service.ts`): the expenses and payments outside the trash as cards, and the history as system lines (`activityLines`), leaving out `expense.created` and `payment.created` (the card says it). The newest `THREAD_LIMIT` (200) together, oldest first, each on its own day: an expense's `spent_on`, a payment's `paid_on`, a line's day in the caller's time zone. A former member's group thread stops when they left. Comments are lines too (Task 92, below).
- **A group's page** `/g/$groupId`: the plate, the name, kind, members and dates, the caller's balance, Add expense (with the group chosen) and `?tab=settings`: the details, default split and simplify (saved at their `version`), the members (add a friend or someone new: `addKharchaNewGroupMember`; invite one without an account; remove one, the owner's), archive (the owner's) and leave. Leaving and removing need a zero balance (the server says the amounts).
- **A friend's page** `/f/$personId`: their balance, Add expense (split with them), a More menu (rename, add an address, invite, hide), the groups both are in with the balance between the two in each (links), and what is between them outside groups.
- **Expenses** on a thread open in the panel over it (`?expense=`, masked as `/e/$expenseId`), as on Spend.

## The split editor (0284)

- **"Split with"** in the expense dialog: nobody, friends (picked as chips), or a group (its current members, and whoever is on an old expense already; a group's expense stays in its group). Choosing a group brings its currency until the caller picks one; the method starts from the group's default split, else the caller's.
- **The editor** (lazy, `split-editor.tsx`) edits a `SplitDraft` of text fields (`split-model.ts`, pure): who paid (one person or several, each an amount), how (equal, amounts, percent, shares, adjust, itemized with the "10% service charge" and "10% service and 13% VAT" presets on the subtotal, one owes all), each person's share live. `buildSplit` gives the core's `Split`, payers, shares and debts, or the first problem; the line under the editor says it ("NPR 500.00 left to assign") or the sentence the split makes ("You paid NPR 3,000.00; Asha owes you NPR 1,000.00."). Each method keeps its own numbers.
- **Keyboard:** every control is native (a select, radios, checkboxes, text fields): Tab between them, arrow keys between methods.
- **Each person's category:** the caller's category is theirs; everyone else on a shared expense gets their own category with the same built-in key (`defaultShareCategories`), on creating and when an edit adds them.

## Balances and settling up (0285)

- **Balances everywhere:** Home's totals (owed and owe per currency) and its "debts to settle" to-dos (each friend the caller owes, in all, `SETTLE_LIMIT` 5); each friend's balance overall in their header and per group in their page; each group's **Balances** tab (`?tab=balances`): every member's net in words ("gets back", "owes"), then how to settle: the fewest payments when the group simplifies (`simplify`), else who owes whom pair by pair (`groupPairs`, both in the members' order: who joined first, then by id). A balance at zero reads "settled up".
- **A group's Totals tab** (`?tab=totals`, `getKharchaGroupTotals`, lazy, its loader prefetches it): per currency, what the group spent (expenses less refunds), each member's share and what they paid, and the caller's own share by their own category. Categories are each person's own, so the group has no shared breakdown by category. A former member's stop when they left. Plain tables in a `ScrollRegion` each, no chart.
- **Settle up** (the + menu, a group's or a friend's header, a suggestion's Settle button, Home's to-do): the lazy `settle/settle-dialog.tsx` over `getKharchaSettleUp` (`settle/service.ts`). Its choices are the open debts as the payments that would clear them: in a group its suggestions (the caller's first); with a friend the balance between the two outside groups, then in each open group both are in; from the + menu every balance the caller has outside groups and each open group's suggestions that name them. "Someone else, or another amount" picks the payer and the recipient (a group's current members, or the caller and their friends). A smaller amount is a partial payment, said in words. Then a method (a label, the caller's methods suggested), a note and a date. The recipient's pay note shows when they wrote one.
- **Another currency:** "Paid in another currency" takes the currency handed over and a typed rate (`RATE_PATTERN`); `convertAt` gives what was handed over, in integers through BigInt, rounded half up to that currency's digits. The debt is settled in its own currency. The server recomputes it and refuses a mismatch or the same currency (`assertPaidAtRate`). The rate comes back with its 8 decimals ("0.62500000").
- **Payments** on a thread open the same dialog to edit (`paymentId`): amount, currency, what was handed over, method, note and date; who paid whom stays (delete and record another). Delete sends it to the trash with an Undo.
- **Remind** (`remindKharchaFriend`): an email (`kharcha-reminder`) to a friend who owes the caller, at their account's address or the one a contact was added with: the amounts per currency, the caller's pay note, a link (the thread for an account, the landing for a contact). In the recipient's language when their profile has one (`kh_profile.locale`, saved by onboarding and the profile), else the sender's. Once a day per friend (`REMIND_WINDOW_MS`, the latest `friend.reminded` line; the sender's profile row is locked while checking), only while `kh_settings.reminders` is on, never from a guest or a sender whose `invites_blocked_at` is set. It writes a `friend.reminded` line in the two people's thread. `FriendThread.remind` says whether the button shows and when the last one went.
- **One primary action:** a group's header shows Settle up while the caller owes there, else Add expense (the other as an outline button); a friend's shows Settle up while the caller owes them, Remind while they owe the caller, else Add expense, the rest in its More menu.
- **Settled states:** a group where everyone is settled, once something happened in it, offers its owner to archive it (`SettledNotice`); a friend at zero reads "settled up".
- **The pay note** is Settings → Profile (`/settings/profile`, `updateKharchaProfile`): with the home currency and the time zone.

## Conversations and notifications (0286)

- **Threads by key:** `g:<group>`, `f:<the other person>`, `e:<expense>`, always from the reader's side (`threadKey`, `parseThread`, `threadPath`). `resolveThread` (`conversations/service.ts`) resolves one through the group's, friend's or expense's resolver: who is in it, whether the caller can write, a former member's cut-off.
- **Comments** (`kh_comment`: the group, the pair or the expense, the author, plain text up to 2,000, the mentioned ids): in a group's and a friend's thread among the lines (by the day they were written), on the expense page and panel under its history. Written by anyone who can write in the thread (a current member of an open group, either friend, anyone on an expense outside the trash), 60 per 10 minutes; edited and deleted by their author only (a deleted one leaves the thread). Reactions: `REACTIONS`, one of each per person, by anyone who can write.
- **Mentions:** typing `@` in the composer lists the thread's people with an account; arrows move, Tab or Enter picks, Escape closes. The text gets `@` and their own name (the handle, never the caller's nickname for them); the server keeps the picked ids that are in the thread. `commentParts` finds links (http and https, `rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow ugc"`) and the mentions when it is drawn.
- **The composer** (`Composer`): the comment, a + that adds an expense right there (in the group, or with the friend), Send or ⌘/Ctrl+Enter.
- **Unread:** lines from others (history and comments) after the read mark (`kh_read`), from joining in a group; `ThreadTalk.unread` on a thread, `PeoplePage.threads` on People. `useMarkRead` marks the thread read once it renders with unread lines (never in a loader: preloading would read it), and that reads its notifications too. Writing a comment reads the thread.
- **People** runs groups and friends by latest activity with an unread badge. **Focus mode** (`kh_profile.focus`, the Focus button) lists only conversations unread or unsettled and says how many it left out.
- **Notifications** (`kh_notification`, `notify/produce.ts`): added to a group, an expense with you added, changed or deleted, a payment to you, a mention, a comment in your thread, a reminder; a budget at 80% or 100% and a recurring expense made (Task 94: in the digest by default). Never the actor, only people with an account, the first event naming someone wins (a mention over the plain comment). Home's **Updates** lists the newest 20 (unread marked, Mark all read, each opens its thread and is read), and the bell in the rail and the phone's bar shows the unread count.
- **Email** (`notify/deliver.ts`, signed Kharcha, in the recipient's `kh_profile.locale`): Settings → Notifications sets each kind to at once, the digest or none (`NOTIFY_DEFAULTS`: at once only for "added you", payments and reminders; only what differs is stored). Instant rows go after the commit, one `kharcha-notification` per recipient and thread, coalesced within five minutes; the daily `kharcha-digest` gathers the rest (and instant rows unsent after ten minutes). Someone who left the group since gets no email about it; guests get none. An account's reminder is a `reminder` notification whose instant email is `kharcha-reminder` itself.
- **People without an account** (`notify/contact-mail.ts`) get only invites and reminders, at most one a day of either (`kh_person.mailed_at`); a reminder that day is refused (RATE_LIMITED), an invite is made but not sent. Each carries `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and a footer link to `/api/kharcha/unsubscribe?p=…&t=…&l=…` (an HMAC of the person with `BETTER_AUTH_SECRET`): POST (the mail app's one click, or the page's button) sets `kh_person.unsubscribed_at`, after which reminders are refused (FORBIDDEN, and no Remind button) and invites aren't sent; GET is a small page of its own that asks first.
- **The daily job** `GET /api/cron/kharcha` (`cron.ts`, 01:30 UTC, `CRON_SECRET`): recurring expenses due and budget alerts (Task 94, first, so the digest carries them), the digests, the trash past `TRASH_DAYS` (receipts soft-deleted for the attachments' clean-up), invites a month past their date, read notifications older than 90 days. Each step idempotent and on its own; the admin's counts (96) join it.

## Budgets (0288)

- **What counts:** the owner's share of each expense outside the trash, a refund against it, in the budget's currency, by their own category (or everything). Periods: weeks from Sunday, calendar months and years, in their time zone (`period.ts`). A budget applies from the period holding `starts_on`; a monthly one can have its own amount for a month (`kh_budget_month`, "November only" in the dialog; empty goes back).
- **One per category, currency and period;** a second is CONFLICT. Deleting a category deletes its budget (`cascade`). 30 each (a guest 10, `quotas.budgets`).
- **How it stands** (`budgetViews`): this period's amount, what was spent, the state (`ok` under 80%, `near`, `over` at 100% and above, in integers: `thresholdsReached`) and the percent, and up to six earlier periods. `BudgetMeter` draws it: a native `<meter>` for screen readers beside a drawn bar (ink, ochre, ochre hatched), the state in words with an icon, what is left or over.
- **Alerts** (`sendBudgetAlerts`, the daily job): each threshold reached in the period holding today is claimed in `kh_budget_alert`; only a claimed one notifies (`budget.alert`, target `b:<budget>`: Insights' budgets), the higher one if both. Nothing claimed, nothing told: two runs, or two at once, send one.
- **Where:** Insights → Budgets (New, Edit, Delete, earlier periods), Home's summary and its to-dos.

## Recurring expenses (0288)

- **"Repeat"** on an expense the caller could edit (the expense page and panel): every n weeks, months or years, until an end date or not. The expense is occurrence 0 (`recurring_n` 0); the next is the first after its day and after today. An expense already repeating is CONFLICT. The template keeps what it is, its payers and split, and the caller's own category, method and tags; everyone else's category comes from the creator's built-in key, as on any new expense.
- **Days** (`cadence.ts`): occurrence n is counted from `starts_on`, so month ends and 29 February stay right (`occurrenceOn`); `firstOnOrAfter` finds the next; `dueOccurrences` what the job owes, at most `CATCH_UP_MAX` (60) a run.
- **The job** (`makeDueRecurring`): candidates are due by tomorrow in UTC, then each is checked against today in its owner's time zone; one transaction each, its row locked; each occurrence through `addCheckedExpense` (group, currency, people, lists, quota, the helpers) as the owner, with "added" for the others on it and `recurring.made` for the owner. Each expense made carries `seq` (unique with the recurring id), which only grows. A refusal pauses it with the reason (`problem`: group, people, quota, currency, split); any other error is logged and tried again the next day.
- **Settings → Recurring** (`RecurringList`): what each makes, how often, the next one (or paused, ended, and why), Skip the next one, Pause and Resume (from the next after today: nothing missed is made), Edit (a new cadence counts from the last one made), Delete (what it made stays). Home lists one made tomorrow.
- **Its owner's alone:** the others on a group's rent see the expenses it makes, never the recurring expense (NOT_FOUND). Leaving the group, or the group being archived, stops it with "group". 30 each (a guest 10).

## Insights (0288)

- **`/insights/$tab`** (`?month=`, `?currency=`, `?from=`, `?to=`; Insights in the rail and the tabs is current on every tab): sums over the caller's shares, one currency at a time (the one asked for if they spent in it, else the home currency, else the largest; a switch when there are several).
- **Overview:** the month's total against the month before, in words; by category (two series, this month and last: the largest eight, then Other); the top eight merchants (the title when there is none) as a ranked list; every day as bars; and a calendar table (weeks from Sunday, one hue in four steps, each cell's amount for screen readers and in its title). The months step back and forward, never past this one.
- **Trends:** twelve months to the chosen one: the totals, then lines by category, by tag (an expense with two tags in both) and shared (a group's expenses by group, others by each other person on them), each the four largest and Other, so the kit's five colours are never cycled.
- **Statement:** a range of days (this month until one is chosen; put in order and kept within two years by `statementOf`): per currency what was spent, the refunds, the two together and how many, by category, and every expense (the first 1,000; the totals count all). Print or save as PDF is the browser's: the shell is `print:hidden` and the page white (`kharcha.css`).
- Charts are the kit's (lazy, with `ChartFrame`'s table); the calendar and the merchants' bars are plain markup.

## Pitfalls already solved

- **A split's people all have shares**, even of zero (0%, one paisa among three): an expense's people are its payers and shares, and `mergePerson` rewrites the ids in `split` through them.
- **Leaving is stamped with the database's clock** (`now()`), as `created_at` is: a former member sees what was made before they left, and nothing made after, even within the same millisecond.
- **Money in an error message** is formatted with a no-break space between the code and the amount ("NPR 6.00"); tests normalize it.
- **Currency names differ between Node's ICU and the browser's in Nepali** (`Intl.DisplayNames`): `currencyLabel` as server-rendered text fails hydration (React #418). Headings show the code; the label is for pickers.
- **An input with a `list` (datalist) is a combobox**, not a textbox, to Playwright and screen readers.
- **Sentry's release snippet** (a build with `SENTRY_*` in `.env`) isn't counted by `check-bundle.ts` (0279).
- **The margin rule is drawn above the lines** (`before:z-10` on `LedgerPage`): each `LedgerLine` is positioned and painted on the page colour, so a rule beneath them disappears.
- **A fix badge truncates** inside its column: a nowrap badge wider than a phone's middle column spilled under the amount beside it.
- **The shell never swaps frames while signing out** (0282): sign-out clears the session before the page is left; a layout that fell back to the public frame remounted Home, its queries ran with no session, and the 401 sent the browser to `/login`. Pages read the caller's id through `useKharchaUserId()`.
- **A modal on load fails the browser review:** the first tab stop must be the skip link, so onboarding is a card, not a dialog.
- **Links on the red header** take the cloth's colours (`text-kh-cloth-muted`, the current one `text-kh-on-cloth`): the shared language switcher's own greys fail contrast there.
- **A text input with a `list` (a `<datalist>`) is a combobox** to assistive tech and to tests: find the merchant with `getByRole("combobox", { name: /Merchant/ })`.
- **Story assertions on numbers use `formatNumber`:** the dark Nepali project draws Devanagari digits.
- **The receipt viewer's scrolling area is a tab stop** (axe's `scrollable-region-focusable`): a zoomed receipt must scroll from the keyboard.
- **A new route file needs the route tree regenerated** before typecheck sees it (`src/routeTree.gen.ts` is the router plugin's): start Vite once (`pnpm exec vite dev`), or build.
- **The invite lookup's rate limit trips the e2e review,** which opens a page dozens of times from one address: its invite pages resolve through `kharcha-invite`, which resets the limits first.
- **The expense dialog keeps its first split draft at once** (state set during render): until then each render made a new draft, whose lines got new keys.
- **A `GROUP BY` on an expression with bound parameters** (the other person of a pair, `case when person_lo = $1 …`) fails in Postgres: the select's `$1` and the group's `$8` are different expressions. `threadStates` groups by position (`group by 1`).
- **A thread is read from the browser, not the loader:** link preloading runs loaders, so a mark there would clear badges nobody saw (`useMarkRead`).
- **An email's Nepali subject must hold Devanagari** (`tests/email/templates.test.ts`): "{heading} · {site}" with an English sample fails; the subject says "मा नयाँ".
- **A field's value that starts with `=`, `<` or `>` is quoted** when printed: `amount>` with `=5` would read back as `amount>=5`.
- **`formSearchPatch` drops empty controls,** so `FilterBar`'s `transform` sees a cleared select as missing: Spend reads a missing control as "" (any).
- **An import makes the default lists first** (`ensureLists`), as every list change does: otherwise a file's "Cash" would become a second Cash once the defaults arrived.
- **A union page orders by the select's aliases** (`"day"`, `"at"`, `"id"` through `sql.identifier`): the two sides' columns are of different tables.
- **Lazy views in stories need time:** the table and the chart load on demand, so their stories wait up to 5 s for them (`findByTestId(…, {}, { timeout: 5000 })`).
- **`.storybook/mocks/kharcha.ts` is the sample data;** the section decorator is `kharcha-section.tsx`, since `kharcha.tsx` would resolve to the same import.
- **The daily job runs as of "now":** tests pass `now` to `makeDueRecurring` and `runKharchaCron` to move time; the e2e flow can't, so the harness moves a recurring expense's days back a month instead.
- **Two people merged into one on a template** (both on the same recurring expense) leave it naming one person twice: its next run fails the split and pauses it ("split") rather than guessing a new split.
- **A meter's own look can't be hatched:** `BudgetBar` keeps a native `<meter>` (visually hidden) for assistive tech and draws the bar beside it; Biome refuses `role="meter"` on a div.
